Frequently asked questions

1. Why should I switch to an electric scooty in 2026?

Say goodbye to rising fuel costs  enjoy a smooth, affordable ride every single day with YJ Future.

2. Can I buy an electric bike on instalments in Pakistan?

Yes! Electric bikes in Pakistan are available on easy instalment plans through Bank Alfalah. You can finance your electric bike with a low down payment and convenient monthly instalments, making it easier for salaried individuals, students, and business owners to own an e-bike without paying the full amount upfront. Simply visit your nearest Bank Alfalah branch or apply online to check your eligibility and get your favourite electric bike today!

3. Which is the most affordable electric bike in Pakistan?

 The YJ Future Indus is currently the most affordable electric bike in Pakistan, priced at approximately PKR 90,000. It’s ideal for students and daily commuters looking for a budget-friendly, eco-friendly alternative to petrol bikes.

4. How long does it take to charge a YJ Future electric bike?

A YJ Future electric bike takes approximately 5 to 6 hours for a full charge using a standard home socket. No special charging station is required — simply plug in overnight and your bike is ready for the next day. This makes it one of the most convenient electric bikes in Pakistan for daily use.

5. What is the battery warranty on YJ Future electric bikes?

YJ Future offers up to an 18-month warranty on the motor, battery, and controller  36 months for lithium battery models. Our bikes use CHILWEE Graphene batteries, known for longer life, faster charging, and higher safety than standard lead-acid batteries.
